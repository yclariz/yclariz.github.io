About

Growing up, I was an avid artist and percussionist. My time spent at the Rochester Institute of Technology has expanded my technical and creative ability, allowing me to further understand the difference between art and design, and integrate them both into my professional work. I have discovered a love for editorial design through my role as a print coordinator and designer for Reporter Magazine, and enjoy incorporating illustration into my work. I love flowers, friends, problem solving, summer, music, and making people laugh.
