Surestep + Dignishield Packaging

At my internship with Becton Dickinson, I was tasked with updating packaging for two lines of medical devices - one, the SureStep Foley Catheter System, and two, the DigniShield Stool Management System. These products are the highest quality of their kind on the market, and they were in desparate need of a redesign. Despite designing several color options for these products and their multiple levels of packaging, we settled on the iteration that included orange accents in order to make them stand out from competitors. The updated designs are consistent, utilize a grid across all systems, are legible to consumers, and fall within medical packaging regulations.
