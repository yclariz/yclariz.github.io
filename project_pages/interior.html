Interior Design Booklet

Each spring, around twenty senior interior design students from the Rochester Institute of Technology finalize and defend their year-long capstone projects. For the years of 2025 and 2026, I was responsible for compiling their work into a small, square booklet. About fifty copies are printed, each acting as a physical way of memorializing an otherwise digital or conceptual project. The template used for each student aims to give the reader a high-level understanding of the students' theses, as well as draw them in with the best snapshots of their final mockups.
