Wegmans Breakfast Foods

Wegmans is a prominent grocery store chain in the Northeast Region of the US. It has over 110 retail locations and over 53,000 employees. However, Wegmans store brand chooses not to feature mascots on their food products - potentially missing out on a key consumer ‘grab’ when targeting families or children.

The Brief

“Redesign an existing breakfast box to include the addition of a character on each box. Additionally, Wegmans is currently striving to remove all artificial dyes from products, corn syrup, and make foods as whole and healthy as possible. The back of the box should call attention to the health-conscious aspects of the product.”


Analysis of Existing Product

Strengths
- Clean, legible design
- Protein is great for breakfast
- Feel full/more satisfied
- Eat healthily throughout the day
- Convenient alternative to fresh eggs
Weaknesses
- Could be unfamiliar to children
- Location in store: Frozen section
- Could actually be an advantage
- Proposed design would stand out in frozen section

Concept

I wanted to bring some familiarity back into the design of the packaging in order to make it more recognizable to children. I thought that a weird (but perhaps not off-putting) chicken could be a fun approach, drawn in a cartoonish style. This chicken mascot can change colors and outfits depending on the variation of the product: the western chicken will be pink with a cowboy hat.
